=== 4. WHY THE NUMBER DIFFERS FROM 24 ===
PR 331's 24 is ONE filter over the WHOLE part at 0.25 x median.
The double tap derives a filter FROM THE TAP, so on this part it
gives 1-32 faces depending on which face is tapped, median 7.
Both numbers are reproduced above from the same part, so neither is
being taken on trust. The 24 is unchanged (24); the
per-seed spread is what a TAP can produce and is new information.
A KIND-ONLY filter's worst seed still matches 36 of 78 faces
(46% of the model) — the wrong answer PR 331 proved wrong, not used here.
