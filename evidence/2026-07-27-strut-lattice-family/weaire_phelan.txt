WEAIRE-PHELAN — attempt & verdict
  The Weaire-Phelan (A15 / Pm-3n) foam IS periodic in a single cubic cell,
  so at first glance it looks like just another table entry. It is not, for
  the current per-cell SEGMENT model, and here is the concrete reason:

  Its 8-cell unit (2 dodecahedra + 6 tetrakaidecahedra) is the VORONOI
  tessellation of the A15 point set
     2a:  (0,0,0), (1/2,1/2,1/2)
     6c:  (1/4,0,1/2) and its 5 axis permutations.
  The strut skeleton is the Plateau-border network of that tessellation:
  its vertices are 4-valent junctions sitting at positions that are NOT the
  corner / edge / face-centre / body-centre dictionary the other nine
  lattices share, and the edges are the Voronoi edges of an 8-seed cell.

  Producing that edge list REQUIRES the seed-Voronoi step this task puts
  OUT OF SCOPE ("Voronoi ... needs seed generation and robust thickening").
  A per-cell integer segment table cannot express it without either (a)
  running that Voronoi construction, or (b) hand-transcribing an arbitrary
  straight-edge approximation of relaxed, slightly-curved Plateau borders.
  Either way it is NEW machinery, not a table row.

  VERDICT: attempted; does NOT fit the current per-cell segment model. Not
  forced. It belongs with the out-of-scope Voronoi generator, alongside the
  TPMS sheets (isosurface generator). Said plainly, as the task asked.
