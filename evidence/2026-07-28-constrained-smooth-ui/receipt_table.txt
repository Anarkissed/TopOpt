THE RECEIPT IS REAL — constrained smoothing weakens a load-bearing part, and the
re-certification reports the LOWER margin (bar S3). Device-real, produced by
core/build/topopt-cli built from this worktree on the synthetic cog specimen.

Specimen: an 8-tooth cog bar (a star cross-section — the teeth are load-bearing
ribs) in axial TENSION. PLA, resolution 48 (1 mm voxels). Anchor = the -X cap
(pseudo-face 20), traction 3550 N on the +X cap (pseudo-face 21). Gate = the
production 1.5 margin stop. A DECLARED EXTERNAL LOAD is the key: unlike self-weight
(a lighter part is always safer, so a margin can only RISE — PR 196/200's wall),
a fixed traction does not fall when material is removed, so rounding the ribs off
LOWERS the margin.

  strength  pairs   peak stress   margin   verdict     min-feat.viol.
  --------  -----   -----------   ------   --------     --------------
   solid       0     33.64 MPa    1.635    ACCEPTED         288
   0.05        1     33.65 MPa    1.635    ACCEPTED         290
   0.10        2     33.65 MPa    1.635    ACCEPTED         292
   0.20        4     49.04 MPa    1.122    REJECTED  <--- the crossing / the flip
   0.40        8     51.77 MPa    1.062    REJECTED          23
   1.00       20     51.61 MPa    1.066    REJECTED          27

  (all rows: gate "required" = 1.5, the production loadcase margin stop.)

Reading it:
  * The SOLID part is ACCEPTED at margin 1.635.
  * GENTLE smoothing (1-2 Taubin pairs) barely moves the ribs — margin holds at
    1.635, still ACCEPTED. (A smooth surface has almost nothing to denoise.)
  * Rounding the ribs off (4+ pairs) shrinks the tension-carrying section, peak
    stress climbs 33.6 -> 49-52 MPa, and the re-analysed margin drops through the
    1.5 stop to ~1.1 — the gate flips to REJECTED. The numbers on screen are the
    SMOOTHED part's, never the solid's (the honesty rule).
  * min-feature VIOLATIONS fall (288 -> 23-32) because smoothing removes the
    terracing — yet the part gets WEAKER. Two independent facts, both reported
    honestly; "less terraced" is not "stronger".

THE MIN-FEATURE HARD CONSTRAINT IS THE SAFETY (last row of the transcript):
  strength 1.00, constraint ON  ->  pairs 0/20 applied, min_feature_limited=1,
  margin 1.635, ACCEPTED.
The constraint REFUSES to round ribs that would thin below the printable floor, so
a user on the default (constraint on) never reaches the REJECTED state — the melt
is structurally impossible, and the receipt says the strength was capped.

Volume drift (bar S4) is reported at every strength against the Taubin bound. Here
it EXCEEDS the small-perturbation bound (~1-5% vs ~0.03-0.57%): rounding real ribs
moves far more material than denoising a smooth surface, and the receipt shows the
TRUE drift — that IS the honest signal ("this was aggressive, not gentle").
