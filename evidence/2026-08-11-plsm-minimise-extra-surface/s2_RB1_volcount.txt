THE ANSWER — every arm against SIMP, rung by rung

The headline amplitude is the CAD population. PR 314 settled that the
CUT surface has no ground truth, so its distance-to-CAD is a fact about
the part's shape and not an error; the CUT column here is therefore the
rms DIHEDRAL, which needs no reference. Sub-voxel content is the
MECHANISM column and is signed the other way up: bigger = more sub-voxel
placement, because a crossing at the edge midpoint IS a staircase.
