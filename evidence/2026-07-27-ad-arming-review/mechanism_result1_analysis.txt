DEEP-JACOBI EVENTS (the cost driver — each is a stagnated V-cycle that bailed to Jacobi
after the 300-cycle budget, then ground thousands of CG iters):
  rec(noAD): 2 events @ iters {3,5} = 5803 + 8004 = 13807 CG
  rec+AD   : 2 events @ iters {3,6} = 6299 + 11987 = 18286 CG   (+4479 in the deep events)

THE MECHANISM, NAMED:
  * The restriction OVERHEAD is negligible: AD only actually restricts iter 2 (active_frac
    0.31), costing +146 CG. Everything else is downstream.
  * The escape latch works AS DESIGNED: the band fights the optimizer's descent for 2
    steps, the optimizer pushes 1008 elements out of the band, the latch detects it at
    iter 3 and reverts to full domain. From iter 3 on, BOTH postures solve the identical
    full-domain operator.
  * The +3980 is TRAJECTORY DIVERGENCE, not overhead. The 2 pre-latch restricted steps
    seed a DIFFERENT design at iter 3. On this job the per-iteration cost is dominated by
    a FEW unpredictable deep Jacobi-stagnation solves (5k-12k CG each); a different design
    path lands a different set of those events. Here it landed a WORSE one: rec's second
    deep solve was 8004 CG (iter 5); AD's was 11987 CG (iter 6).
  * So AD cannot deliver a reliable win in this regime. Its dilution mechanism (shrink the
    domain to cut Jacobi work) never operates sustainedly — the latch turns it off after 2
    iters — and the residual effect is chaotic trajectory noise. The SIGN of that noise is
    not controlled; here it is +26%, but the point is it is a coin-flip on a chaos-
    sensitive stagnating trajectory, paid on top of AD's setup + O(N) escape detection.

Contrast with the HEALTHY regime (draft-arming A5, 16x8x16, no stagnation): there the band
never latches (f_bar 0.58) and rec+AD = 4062 vs rec = 4878 CG — AD saves 17%, because every
solve is cheap multigrid and shrinking the domain shrinks each cheap solve proportionally.
The win exists ONLY where multigrid carries every solve; it inverts to a loss the moment the
job stagnates (which is exactly the 128^3 design-box regime AD was armed FOR).
