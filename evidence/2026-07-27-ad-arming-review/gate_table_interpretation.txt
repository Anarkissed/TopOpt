READING (what the gate table establishes for the recommendation):
  1. DISARMING AD IS GATE-SAFE. OFF and ON produce IDENTICAL verdicts and an IDENTICAL
     terminal recommendation. Switching the armed default ON->OFF changes nothing the
     gate decides. OFF is additionally byte-identical to the pre-187 reference world
     that reproduces 185/197 to the digit (THE ONE RULE: band 0 derives no mask).
  2. THE ARMED POSTURE IS ALREADY THE WORSE ONE ON THIS GATE FIXTURE. Even on a HEALTHY
     46.5x fixture, ON costs +9.8% CG and MISSES the 0.1% margin bar on rung 1 (dM/M
     1.097e-3 vs OFF), because rung 1's trajectory is mildly divergent: the escape latch
     fires @iter3 (50 escapes) and AD pays the restriction + trajectory-divergence cost
     without the sustained-restriction benefit. This is the SAME mechanism as result 1,
     in miniature, on the gate fixture itself.
  3. AD's healthy-regime WIN is FRAGILE: it materialises only when the trajectory is
     healthy AND never trips the escape latch (ARM8 -17%, ARM16 -25%). The moment the
     latch fires (this gate fixture, ARM12, big-stag) AD is a net LOSS. Production cannot
     choose the fixtures it runs; the maintainer's target (128^3, deep stagnation) is
     the loss end.
