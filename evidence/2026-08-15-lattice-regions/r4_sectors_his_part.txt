★ WHAT WORKS: four region-backed lattice regions resolved from VOXEL MASKS
  on his own part, each with its OWN verdict row, and the declared depth
  drives SELECTION monotonically — 308 -> 372 -> 522 -> 781 candidate
  voxels. Every one latticed (latticed == candidate, 0 solid fallback).

★ WHAT DOES NOT: extent_mm is 3.4106 in ALL FOUR — EXACTLY 2 x the 1.70528 mm
  spacing — so one cell, one density, one strut, distinct_cells = 1.
  THE DEPTHS DO NOT GRADE THE LATTICE. Per §4(b): if the sectors do not
  differ, the feature does not work whatever the unit tests say.

★ ROOT CAUSE, and it is my own §1(c) choice, not a wiring slip:
  region_thinnest_extent_mm took the MINIMUM of the Hildebrand thickness
  over the region. The largest ball that fits inside a set and contains a
  voxel ON THAT SET'S BOUNDARY is ~1-2 voxels however thick the set is
  elsewhere — so the minimum is a CONSTANT by construction and cannot
  discriminate a 2-layer sector from a 4-layer one. It is not noisy; it is
  degenerate. I argued for it over a percentile on conservatism grounds and
  the argument was right about conservatism and wrong about usefulness.
