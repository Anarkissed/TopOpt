certification of a DIFFERENT part on a DIFFERENT grid. So it is measured
here, on HIS part, through the shipped seam, at three resolutions, on the
SAME object each time: rung 0.68 as it is exported today.

  topopt-cli analyze job_analyze<res>.json --mesh meshes/v068_exported.stl

CG iteration count only on NON-convergence (analyze.hpp:33-35), so a
converged certification surfaces wall clock and peak RSS and no iteration
