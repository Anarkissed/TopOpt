S4 — WHAT A FINER CERTIFICATION ACTUALLY COSTS, MEASURED ON HIS PART.

The task says not to price this from PR 299's 47.44 s — that is a
certification of a DIFFERENT part on a DIFFERENT grid. Measured here,
through the shipped seam, at three resolutions:

  topopt-cli analyze job_analyze<res>.json --out /tmp/s4out

NO --mesh. Not a simplification — a FINDING. The first attempt certified
the SAME exported mesh at each resolution and the seam REFUSED it at 192
and at 256 with the same under-constrained error the SDF mesh gets
(s4_mesh_substitution_refused.txt): re-voxelizing a mesh onto a grid
finer than the one it came from leaves load-tagged voxels empty. So the
cost of a certification at resolution R is measured on the object that
CAN be certified at any R — the part itself.

Wall AND, where it is recorded, iterations. FixedDesignAnalysis records the
CG iteration count only on NON-convergence (analyze.hpp:33-35), so a
CONVERGED certification surfaces wall clock and peak RSS and no iteration
count. No figure is invented for it.

### resolution 256
  STOPPED, NOT COMPLETED — and the reason is the answer.

  The 256 run allocated a peak of 1.86 GB (observed live) and then fell to about
  2% duty: 20 minutes of CPU accumulated over more than two hours of wall clock,
  at 93% of ONE core. This Mac has 16 GB and was hosting two unrelated
  optimization runs from other worktrees; at the point this was stopped the
  system had 8,908 pages free (139 MB) with 1.9 million pages in the compressor.
  The certification was not compute-bound, it was MEMORY-bound, and it was still
  running after the 128 and 192 rows had both finished.

  So the honest 256 figure is not a number of seconds. It is: at 3.7 million
  elements this certification does not fit in a 16 GB machine alongside anything
  else, and the 128 -> 192 scaling above (3.4x the elements, 6.0x the CPU,
  2.4x the memory) is the measurement to reason from.
======================================================================
(This Mac's cores AND memory were shared with two unrelated optimization runs in
 other worktrees for this measurement, so the wall figures are an UPPER bound;
 peak RSS is unaffected by sharing.)
