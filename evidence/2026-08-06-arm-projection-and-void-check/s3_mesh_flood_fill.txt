★ THE COUNT IS SWEPT ACROSS THE MEASURING GRID, and that is not a formality.
The flood fill discretises the exported mesh, so its OWN grid can close a
channel that the mesh leaves open — and it does. On the first pass, at a single
grid, this probe reported a 703-voxel / 6960.668 mm^3 "cavity" appearing on rung
068 of the resolution-64 run when projection was armed. That looked exactly like
the blocked-stop this section exists to catch. It is not one: the same two
meshes measured at 96, 128 and 160 report ZERO cavities in both arms. The
channel is narrower than a 3.4 mm measuring voxel, and nothing more.

So a cavity count at one grid is not evidence. Only a count that SURVIVES a
change of grid is, and the sweep below is what separates them.
