R3  THE TRAJECTORY CURVE AND ITS SETTLING ITERATION, per arm per rung

★ WHAT THIS IS AND IS NOT. R3 asks for the certified MARGIN as a curve.
A per-iteration margin curve is not affordable here and the reason is
measured, not asserted: PR 326 timed certifying an UNCONVERGED design at
26x the cost of certifying a converged one, so a margin curve over a
4-rung ladder would cost more than every arm in this table put together
and would put the instrument inside the timing the arms report. What is
printed instead is (a) the per-iteration COMPLIANCE curve, which is free
— it is already in iterations.csv — with the iteration at which it
settles to within 0.1%, and (b) the CERTIFIED margin at every rung, which
is the number the accept gate actually reads. The gap between them is
real and is named in the handoff.
