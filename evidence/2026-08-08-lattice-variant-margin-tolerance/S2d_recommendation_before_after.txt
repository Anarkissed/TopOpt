THE TRADE-OFF THIS MAKES, STATED

  PR 311 noted that the lightest lattice sits on the LARGEST mesh, so ranking by
  printed mass systematically points at the least transferable rung. That is
  still true and is now the shipped behaviour: the recommended object is the one
  he would print, and it is also the 1.95 GB file. On his iPad neither the 740 MB
  nor the 1.95 GB rung can be DISPLAYED (PR 311 §4), so this changes which object
  Export writes, not what he can look at.
