★★ TWO CAVEATS, BOTH REAL, NEITHER SWEPT UNDER:

(1) THE 0.28 mm WELD PITCH IS NOT ACCURATE FOR THESE STRUT SIZES, AND IT ERRS IN BOTH
    DIRECTIONS. Struts here are 0.42-0.86 mm, i.e. 1.5-3 raster voxels. Marching cubes
    cannot represent that faithfully: doubled's welded volume (14,649.9) comes out ABOVE
    its own soup figure (13,565.6), which is impossible for a true union and is the
    raster rounding thin struts UP; organic's comes out BELOW (3,713.6 vs 5,764.8), part
    real overlap-deduction and part rounding DOWN. Only the DEFAULT pitch (half the
    thinnest strut's radius) is trustworthy for a volume, and that is the row starred
    above. Reported rather than quietly using whichever number flattered.

(2) THE MASS COLUMN IS NOT A LIKE-FOR-LIKE COMPARISON BETWEEN ALGORITHMS, and the 4x gap
    between organic and the other two is NOT a claim that organic is four times lighter
    for the same lattice. The band means different things to each law: the octet path
    takes its strut diameter from the MEASURED octet table at the chosen cell, while
    organic derives its bead from the mass coupling and then calibrates it to the length
    actually traced. Both honour the same intent and the same band; they do not produce
    the same solid fraction from it. Comparing them properly needs a matched-density
    experiment nobody has run.
