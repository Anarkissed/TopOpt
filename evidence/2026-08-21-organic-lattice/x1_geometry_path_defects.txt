★★ THE GEOMETRY PATH HAS FOUR DEFECTS THE ANALYZE-PATH BARS COULD NOT CATCH.
Found only because the maintainer sliced the files I sent and said "I don't see any
lattice". He was right, and the checks I had run could not have told me.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
0. WHAT I SENT, AND WHY IT LOOKED SOLID
The three l-bracket STLs contain a lattice of 63 voxels out of 12,703 candidates
(0.5 %); the other 12,640 stayed SOLID. So each file is the solid part plus a handful of
struts rattling inside it, and a slicer — which unions the soup — sees a solid block.
All three files measure 51,231 / 51,275 / 51,293 mm^3: a 0.12 % spread, i.e. the same
object three times.

★ THE CHECKS I RAN COULD NOT SEE THIS. protruding_vertices = 0, file size, triangle
count and watertightness are all satisfied by a solid block. THE CHECK THAT WOULD HAVE
CAUGHT IT IS ONE DIVISION: latticed_voxels / region_voxels. I never ran it on the
geometry path, only on the analyze path, where it reads 76 %.

Cause on that fixture: `strut_unprintable_at_every_cell = 12640` at the synthetic
0.12 mm bead I chose to reach the code path. Not a lattice defect — but I presented the
files as a demonstration of three algorithms and they demonstrate nothing.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. ★ THE R10 CANDIDATE SET IS NOT HIS DECLARED REGIONS
The analyze receipt grades with `region = nullptr` (run_job.cpp), so EVERY printed voxel
is a candidate and his declared include/exclude regions are ignored. That is PR 344's
behaviour, not this task's — but it means R10's "76.3 % latticed" describes latticing the
WHOLE PART, not the regions he selected.

Through the geometry path, WITH his regions, on his real part at his real 0.42 mm bead:
    region 11,002   LATTICED 1,180 (10.7 %)   fallback 9,822
    strut volume 4,716 mm^3 against a 544,112 mm^3 solid companion
The comparison BETWEEN the three algorithms still stands — they all ran on the identical
candidate set — but it is not "his job as he would run it".

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2. ★ STEPPED REFUSES ON A SOLID BLOCK: THE WIDTH FILTER DROPS +inf
`run_stepped_step` keeps a voxel's member width only when `std::isfinite(width[e])`. But
`local_member_thickness_mm` returns +inf where the member exceeds the EDT cap
(thickness_cap_voxels = 32), and the centre of a 40 mm cube at h = 0.625 mm is EXACTLY
32 voxels from the surface. So every interior voxel is dropped, the region has "no
measurable member width", and stepped refuses a part it should lattice trivially.

The fix is the one grading.cpp already uses: treat the cap as a LOWER BOUND on the width
rather than as no measurement. NOT DONE.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
3. ★ ORGANIC ON A DENSE PART: 2.76 GB, AND 27x THE PART'S OWN VOLUME
40 mm cube, fully latticed, aesthetic band [0.1, 0.35]:
    emitted STL          2,764,064,684 bytes  (~55 million triangles)
    interior_volume_mm3  1,752,994  in a 64,000 mm^3 cube  = 27x the cube
    wall                 487.70 s   against doubled's 50.03 s on the same job
The struts overlap so heavily the result is solid, emitted as tens of millions of
interpenetrating cylinders. `organic_strut_width_mm: 1.2` was stated in the job and the
separation still collapsed — and I CANNOT CONFIRM what bead the law used, because of
defect 4.

Contributing cause, measured by sampling the running process: the tracer spends its time
in the proximity query (`nearest_dist`). Bucket cell = the largest separation, so as the
separation shrinks relative to the part the points per bucket grow as 1/d^3 and every RK4
step tests all 27 buckets. `max_curves` and `max_steps_per_curve` bound the curve count;
NOTHING bounds the query cost. On his part (3,056 curves, 2.7-5.1 mm separation) the
tracer is 0.22 s. On this cube it is minutes.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
4. ★ THE GEOMETRY PATH'S RECEIPT DOES NOT NAME THE ALGORITHM
`run_info.grading.algorithm` is absent on `lattice_variant` / `lattice_part` runs, and so
are the whole `organic` and `stepped` blocks. `fill_organic_run_info` and
`fill_stepped_run_info` were wired into `analyze_job`'s RunInfo only. So the one path that
actually writes geometry is the one path whose receipt cannot say which algorithm wrote
it — which is exactly what defeated diagnosis of defect 3. NOT DONE.
