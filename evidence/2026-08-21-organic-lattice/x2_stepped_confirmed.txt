★ IS STEPPED ACTUALLY WORKING? — the maintainer asked, and the answer needed three runs.

WHAT HAD BEEN SHOWN, AND WHY IT WAS NOT ENOUGH.
The cube run reported `regions 1, cells 4.000..4.000, adjacent_region_pairs 0`. Stepped
RAN and emitted — but with ONE region it derives ONE cell, which is a uniform-cell
lattice. Nothing was stepped. Its distinguishing behaviour was UNCONFIRMED, and I had
also worked around a refusal by deleting the region from the job rather than finding out
why it refused. Both of those are exactly what a reviewer should not have to discover.

RUN 1 — STEPPED ON HIS OWN PART, GEOMETRY PATH, HIS FIVE DECLARED REGIONS.
  topopt-cli lattice-variant jobs/part_stepped.json --out part/stepped_real   (300.6 s)

    regions               2
    cells                 8.867 mm and 2.728 mm      <- GENUINELY DIFFERENT, verbatim
    rho per region (FEA)  0.102  and 0.350           <- FEA-driven, per region
    cells/member          5 and 5
    voxels per region     960 and 321
    adjacent pairs        0, joined 0

★ STEPPED WORKS. Two declared regions, two distinct cells derived from each region's own
FEA density and member width, taken VERBATIM with no dyadic snap. That is the algorithm
doing what it is for.

★ AND ITS SEAM COST IS 0 HERE, WHICH IS NOT THE SAME CLAIM AS "NO SEAM COST". The two
latticed regions (960 and 321 voxels at his 0.42 mm bead) do not touch on this part, so
there is no seam to be disconnected. The "4 of 5 abutting pairs disconnected" figure in
r10 comes from the ANALYZE path, where the candidate set is the whole printed part
(region = nullptr) and far more regions abut. Both numbers are real; they are measured
over different candidate sets and must not be quoted as one.

RUN 2 + 3 — THE REFUSAL I HAD WORKED AROUND.
Adding a declared face region to the cube made stepped refuse:
  "the stepped algorithm derived no region cell..."
The message named stepped, so I had assumed stepped. Running DOUBLED on the identical
job settles it:

    DOUBLED with the same declared region: region_voxels 0  LATTICED 0  fallback 0

★ THE REGION MATCHES ZERO VOXELS, FOR EVERY ALGORITHM. It is a malformed test fixture of
mine (a face slab at z = 40 with normal +z), not a code defect, and stepped was reporting
someone else's empty candidate set under its own name.

WHAT WAS FIXED AS A RESULT: the refusal now reports what it MEASURED —
    "Measured: 0 latticed voxels carrying 0 distinct region ids (0 of them with NO id),
     and 0 region(s) had no measurable member width."
A refusal that cannot name its own cause sent me chasing the region resolution when the
fault was upstream of both. With the counts in the message it took two minutes.
