2. APP — THE ONE SHIPPED VALUE THAT COULD HAVE MOVED SILENTLY
==================================================================

ViewerMeshSignature.contentHash is the viewer's upload cache key. Adding
topologyHash beside it must not perturb it by one bit, or every viewer in
the app would decide differently about when to re-upload a mesh.

Method: the BASE tree's struct and this branch's struct compiled SIDE BY
SIDE — both lifted verbatim from their trees, neither retyped — and run
over the same pseudo-random meshes.

4. WHAT THIS BRANCH DELIBERATELY DOES CHANGE (not byte-identical,
   and it would be dishonest to bury these under a checksum)

     camera or restarts the settle. That is S1a, and it is the point. It
     applies to any same-topology swap, so the Original/Smoothed toggle
     keeps the view too. A different part, a different variant or a
     re-meshing still reframes — asserted by
     testANewPartStillReframesTheCamera / testANewPartDoesSettleAgain.

  b. The Smoothed tab is enabled by a preview, not only by a certification
     (S3d).

  c. The receipt drawer gains one footnote (S3e).

  d. The live preview no longer opens the variant's STL (S1b). Same
     geometry in, same geometry out — see section 3.
