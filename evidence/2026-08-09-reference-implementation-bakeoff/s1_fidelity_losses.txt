S1.b — WHAT DOES NOT SURVIVE THE CROSSING
=========================================
Task 2026-08-09-reference-implementation-bakeoff, bar S1(b): "STATE THE FIDELITY
LOSS EXPLICITLY. A comparison whose differences are unlisted is not a
comparison."

Ten differences between his run and the GridapTopOpt arm. They are ordered by
how much they could move the answer, not by how easy they were to find. Three
of them (1, 3, 7) are STRUCTURAL — no amount of work on the driver script
removes them, because the method itself has no place to put the constraint.


WHAT DOES CROSS, EXACTLY
------------------------
Everything in this list is core's own, byte for byte, out of
`build_production_loadcase` (s1_positive_control.txt):

  the grid            128 x 31 x 118, spacing 1.7052793026343613 mm,
                      origin (-18.27575073719824, -48.909974716, 0)
  the discretisation  129 x 32 x 119 nodes, 1,473,696 displacement DOFs —
                      the SAME number his run_info records as solved_grid_dofs
  the part            110,904 solid voxels (23.69% of the box)
  the clamp           21,000 Dirichlet DOFs = 7,000 nodes x 3 components
  the load            7,382 nodal entries, all z, resultant -22.24113464 N
  the frozen set      40,216 FrozenSolid voxels; 70,688 Active
  the material        PLA, E = 3500 MPa, nu = 0.33
  the ladder          [0.68, 0.52, 0.38, 0.26], part-relative


1. ★ THE PART EXTERIOR CANNOT BE REMOVED FROM THE FEA. (structural)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
core marks the 357,320 non-part voxels FrozenVoid and they contribute NO ELEMENT
to the stiffness matrix. A level-set method on a Cartesian background cannot do
that: the level set has to be defined, evolved and reinitialised over the whole
box, and the operator has to stay non-singular there, which is what the ersatz
material is for. In the GridapTopOpt arm the exterior therefore carries
`ϵ = 1e-3` of PLA's modulus — 76.31% of the box at one part in a thousand.

This is not a driver-script choice. Removing the exterior would mean cutting the
background mesh, which is the Unfitted/CutFEM formulation — a different code
path (§S2.4), a different set of examples, and one that needs a conforming
surface mesh of his part to cut with.

WHAT IT COSTS: the exterior is a soft continuum bridging every gap in his part.
It cannot carry much, but it is not nothing, and it makes the level-set arm's
compliance a lower bound on the same design's compliance in core.


2. THE LOAD IS DISTRIBUTED DIFFERENTLY WITHIN THE SAME VOXELS.
---------------------------------------------------------------
core spreads the group's 22.24113464 N as a consistent distributed traction over
the EXPOSED FACES of the 5,165 Load-tagged voxels (`topopt::traction_loads`),
which lands as 7,382 distinct nodal forces ranging from -0.00088 N to -0.00527 N.
The GridapTopOpt arm applies a statically equivalent BODY FORCE over the same
5,165 voxels: `l(v,φ) = ∫ χ_load (v ⋅ g) dΩ`, g = (0, 0, -22.24113464 / V_load).

Same support, same resultant, same line of action. Different distribution
inside the loaded region, so the local stress field differs there. The reason it
is not carried exactly: `AffineFEStateMap` takes the load as a WEAK FORM, and a
prescribed nodal vector is not one. Assembling the right-hand side by hand and
handing it in would mean bypassing their state map, which is the part of the
library the adjoint and the AD both run through.


3. ★ min_feature_mm = 2.5 HAS NO COUNTERPART. (structural)
------------------------------------------------------------
core enforces a minimum printable feature with a density filter of radius
`physical_filter_radius(min_feature_mm, spacing)` and then COUNTS the violations
on the extracted mesh (`min_feature_now`), which is one of the accept gate's
inputs.

GridapTopOpt has two length scales, and neither is that:
  * `η = 2 max(el_Δ)`, the interpolation bandwidth — how wide the ersatz ramp is
    across the boundary. It smooths the integrand, not the geometry.
  * `α = 4 max_steps γ max(el_Δ)`, the Hilbertian extension radius — how far a
    shape sensitivity is smeared before it moves the boundary. It regularises
    the VELOCITY, so it damps high-frequency boundary motion; it does not bound
    the thickness of what the boundary encloses.

A level-set method can and does produce members thinner than either. The arm's
min-feature violation count is therefore MEASURED and reported (it is one of PR
306's controls and is in every row of the table), but it is not CONSTRAINED, and
his run's was.


4. margin_stop = 1.5 HAS NO COUNTERPART, so no level-set rung is certified.
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
His ladder's accept/reject is core's certification: `analyze_fixed_design`
(core/include/topopt/analyze.hpp:306) solves the fixed design under the declared
load, computes a worst-case stress margin under the knockdown, and rejects the
rung below 1.5. GridapTopOpt minimises compliance under a volume constraint. It
has no stress functional in this formulation and no accept gate.

CONSEQUENCE FOR THE TABLE: the `accepted`, `margin_worst_case`,
`margin_effective` and `max_von_mises_mpa` columns are EMPTY for every
GridapTopOpt row, and the probe prints "NOT CERTIFIED BY CORE" rather than a
zero. A smoother surface at an uncertified margin is not the same product as
his, and §S4b says what re-certifying one would take.


5. THE INTERLAYER KNOCKDOWN DOES NOT CROSS.
--------------------------------------------
`z_knockdown` 0.55, `infill_percent` 35, `wall_loops` 5,
`wall_line_width_mm` 0.45 / `wall_line_width_outer_mm` 0.42 all enter the
knockdown spec, and the knockdown only enters the certification. Since (4) does
not cross, neither does this. The two arms are therefore comparing GEOMETRY, not
printability.


6. THE LATTICE GRADING LAW DOES NOT CROSS.
-------------------------------------------
`grade_lattice` (core/include/topopt/grading.hpp:442) takes a per-voxel density
vector and a per-voxel demand field. Nothing downstream of the optimizer is run
on the GridapTopOpt designs in S2; S3 is where a lattice is put on one.


7. ★ THE FROZEN-SOLID SET IS HELD BY A DIFFERENT MECHANISM, AND NOT EXACTLY.
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
core pins a FrozenSolid voxel's density to 1 and excludes it from the update:
40,216 voxels that the optimizer simply cannot touch.

GridapTopOpt has no mask on the design variable. The nearest thing in its own
idiom — the one its `elastic_compliance_ALM.jl` uses to hold Gamma_N still — is a
zero Dirichlet condition on the VELOCITY EXTENSION, so the shape sensitivity is
never allowed to move the boundary there. This arm pins the velocity at every
node that does not touch an Active cell (396,797 + 7,000 of his 491,232 nodes)
AND multiplies the frozen region's stiffness by 1 regardless of φ.

That is exact in the INTERIOR of the pad and inexact at its boundary, and the
reinitialiser is free to move φ anywhere it likes on every iteration. So the
arm reports two controls per rung rather than asserting the constraint held:

    frozen-solid cells still solid       (the whole pad, cell-averaged)
    pad INTERIOR still solid             (pad cells all six of whose
                                          neighbours are also pad)

If the second is not 100%, the constraint was not carried and the row says so.


8. THE OPTIMIZER CANNOT NUCLEATE HOLES, SO IT IS GIVEN SOME.
-------------------------------------------------------------
SIMP forms holes anywhere by driving a density down. A level set can only move
a boundary it already has, so every level-set run needs a seeded initial
topology. This arm uses GridapTopOpt's own `initial_lsf`, scaled to his units
(§S2.3) to put a hole every 8 voxels = 13.64 mm. His run has no such parameter
and no such choice.


9. SIMP p = 3 vs A SHARP INTERFACE.
------------------------------------
Not a loss, a difference in kind, and the one the whole task is about. core's
design variable is a density in [0,1] penalised toward the ends; the level set's
is a boundary position. The `crossing_rms_mm` column measures exactly this: how
much sub-voxel placement each field actually carries.


10. THE LADDER IS WARM-STARTED IN BOTH ARMS, BUT FROM DIFFERENT THINGS.
------------------------------------------------------------------------
His run has `warm_start_inherit: true` — rung k+1 starts from rung k's converged
density. The GridapTopOpt arm seeds rung k+1 from rung k's converged φ, which is
the same idea in the other representation. Reported per rung, since it changes
the iteration counts R4 asks for.
