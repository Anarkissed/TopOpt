2. ★ 0.4293 mm IS THE CAD POPULATION, NOT THE CUT POPULATION
-------------------------------------------------------------
The brief says: "SIMP's rung-068 numbers are 0.4293 mm amplitude and 7.55 deg
rms dihedral on the cut surface."

Those two numbers come from two DIFFERENT populations of PR 319's table:

    obl_cad_rms_mm    0.429291   <- the CAD population.   THIS is the 0.4293.
    obl_cut_rms_mm    3.745211   <- the CUT population.
    dihedral_cut_deg  7.552074   <- the CUT population.   THIS is the 7.55.

So the amplitude figure quoted as "on the cut surface" is the amplitude on the
CAD surface, and the cut surface's own amplitude is 3.7452 mm — 8.7x larger.

THIS IS NOT A TYPO IN THE TABLE; the two columns measure different things on
purpose, and PR 314 is why. On the optimizer-CUT population there is no ground
truth: those vertices are surface the optimizer invented, the CAD says nothing
about where they should be, and their distance to the nearest CAD triangle is a
fact about the part's shape rather than an error. That is why PR 319 reports the
cut population's roughness as an rms DIHEDRAL, which needs no reference, and
keeps the amplitude column on the CAD population, where "how much staircase is
there" has a truthful answer.

  RESOLUTION: the bar is read column by column, and both are reported for every
  arm — amplitude on the CAD population, dihedral on the CUT population — which
  is exactly what PR 319's table does and what §S4a answers against.
