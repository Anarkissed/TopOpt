=== THE THREE CLEANUPS MADE AFTER THE S2 BINARY WAS PINNED, PROVED NEUTRAL ===

WHY THIS FILE EXISTS. The S2 numbers were produced by one binary, whose sha256 is
in s2_binary_of_record.txt and which BOTH arms ran. Three cleanups landed in
simp.cpp after that binary was built:

  * the dead `SemdotField semdot_last` carried across the iteration loop — a full
    copy of the volume-fraction vector (468,224 doubles, 3.7 MB) per iteration,
    assigned and never read;
  * `xphys = std::move(sf.volume_fraction)` in the trajectory block;
  * `xafter = std::move(sf.volume_fraction)` in the post-step block, with the
    achieved-volume read moved above it.

So the committed source does not byte-match the binary that produced the S2
table. Asserting "these are obviously neutral" is the kind of claim this project
has been burned by, so it is MEASURED: the SEMDOT smoke job was re-run through the
rebuilt binary and its design.bin compared with the one the pinned binary wrote.

  before (pinned binary)   90de90bcc3b67afd20c949253e2cffde40c7b8638b001aa7057a403ddba39eb4
  after  (rebuilt binary)  90de90bcc3b67afd20c949253e2cffde40c7b8638b001aa7057a403ddba39eb4
  IDENTICAL

The job is r1_byteid/job_semdot.json (l-bracket, resolution 40, two rungs, SEMDOT
armed at grid_points 4), which exercises the map on every iteration of both rungs
and both of the moved assignments. Re-run: r1_byteid/neutrality_semdot/.
