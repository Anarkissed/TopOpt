A — WHERE run_info.wall_line_width_outer_mm GETS ITS VALUE
==========================================================
The chain, front to back, on the job.json (CLI / LAN worker) path. Every line
below is quoted from the tree at HEAD (commit 90e9ec5, main).

ANSWER: it is an ECHO of MinimizePlasticOptions, and on this path that struct
NEVER RECEIVES the job's outer width. The job's 0.42 is parsed, validated, and
then dropped at ONE line-less spot: core/src/cli/run_job.cpp:3138, where the
job->ProductionLoadCase transfer copies wall_loops and the INNER width and stops.
The echo then mirrors the inner width (0.45) because the mirror sentinel is
still in place.
