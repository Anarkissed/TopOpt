=== ★ THE BEHAVIOUR A USER MUST KNOW: A STATED DENSITY REPLACES THE GRADING ===

Look at the CONTROL's rho range: 0.1386 .. 0.8999 — the WHOLE certifiable
band. That is the grading law doing its job, ramping density with stress
across the region. The OVERRIDE's range is 0.2500 .. 0.6000: EXACTLY the two
stated numbers and nothing between them.

So a stated density does NOT cap, scale, or bias the stress-driven grading.
It REPLACES it inside that region, which becomes UNIFORM at the stated value.
That is what "dial this sector to 25 %" has to mean to be worth having, and
it is what the law does — the branch returns the stated value and never
reaches the demand term. But it is a real trade the user is making: they are
giving up per-voxel stress grading inside that region in exchange for saying
what the region weighs. Both are legitimate; only one is what they asked for.

Corroborating number: density_raised_for_print_voxels is 16792 in the CONTROL
and 0 in the OVERRIDE. In the control, the stress-graded density fell below
printable in 16,792 voxels and the law raised them to the 0.42 mm floor (hence
the control's 0.4200 mm minimum strut). With 0.25 and 0.60 stated, nothing
needed raising — and nothing was silently changed.

=== MASS ===
The override is HEAVIER at every rung (+29.2 g, +6.6 % at 068), which is the
honest direction: 0.25 and 0.60 are both above the 0.1386 the derivation
picks, so asking for them asks for material. The point of the feature is not
to save mass; it is to put the mass WHERE THE USER WANTS IT.
