R3 — THE CAD-PROJECTED GEOMETRY IS UNCHANGED BY A DENSITY OVERRIDE.

=== THE MEASUREMENT ===
The §4(b) arms — identical jobs but for two "relative_density" keys — export
BYTE-IDENTICAL variant meshes on every rung:
    variant_026.stl  IDENTICAL      variant_052.stl  IDENTICAL
    variant_038.stl  IDENTICAL      variant_068.stl  IDENTICAL
and, upstream of them, design.bin / fields.bin / loadcase.json are identical
too. Layer 1 — the CAD face identity used for projection — is untouched to
the byte by a density override.

=== ★ THE TRAP I FELL INTO, AND THE CONTROL THAT MATTERS ===

I first built the control as "same job, project_cad_faces on vs off" by
ADDING "project_cad_faces": true to one arm and leaving the key absent in
the other. The two runs came out byte-identical and I concluded projection
was a NO-OP and that R3 would pass vacuously.

THAT WAS BACKWARDS. output.project_cad_faces is ARMED BY DEFAULT — core's
own test_default_arming.cpp says an ABSENT key must mean ARMED — so I had
compared a configuration WITH ITSELF. Verified directly: the explicit-true
run is byte-identical to a key-absent run (variant_026 and variant_068).

  ⇒ Both §4(b) arms had CAD projection ARMED AND RUNNING. Their identical
    meshes are therefore R3's answer, not an artefact of a disabled feature.

=== POSITIVE CONTROL 1: IS PROJECTION CAPABLE OF MOVING THIS PART? ===
cad_probe re-runs the exact operation run_job runs — attribute_to_cad_faces
then project_onto_cad_faces, with cad_project_options_for_grid(1.7053) — on
the control arm's own exported mesh:

    part          face_count=78  faces.size()=78  faces_are_fitted=0
    variant mesh  41070 vertices, 27381 triangles
    options       tolerance_mm=1.7053  (from spacing 1.7053)
    attribution   attributed=29679  ambiguous=472  seam=7059  of 41070
    displacement  moved=20084 vertices  max=1.7006 mm  mean(moved)=0.0449696 mm
    VERDICT       projection is ACTIVE — R3 can discriminate on this job

  78 B-rep faces, not fitted; 29,679 of 41,070 vertices attributed; the
  operation displaces 20,084 of them, up to 1.70 mm. Projection is a live,
  large operation on this part — not a no-op that would make R3 vacuous.

=== POSITIVE CONTROL 2: AN EXPLICIT project_cad_faces=false ARM — MEASURED ===
The only honest on/off control: the same job with "project_cad_faces": false,
full production ladder, rc=0 at 01:59:17. Against the armed runs:

    variant_026.stl  DIFFERS      variant_052.stl  DIFFERS
    variant_038.stl  DIFFERS      variant_068.stl  DIFFERS

Projection demonstrably SHAPED the bytes R3 compares. Turning it off changes
every exported mesh; changing a region's density changes none of them.

=== VERDICT — R3 MET ===
With CAD projection ARMED (the default) and DEMONSTRABLY ACTIVE — proven two
ways, by the displacement measurement above and by an explicit off arm that
produces different files — applying a per-region density override leaves the
CAD-projected mesh BYTE-IDENTICAL on every rung.

The three-way result in one place:
    projection OFF        vs armed            ->  DIFFERS   (projection is live)
    override vs control, both armed           ->  IDENTICAL (R3's answer)
    override vs control, upstream artifacts   ->  IDENTICAL (design.bin, fields.bin,
                                                             loadcase.json)
