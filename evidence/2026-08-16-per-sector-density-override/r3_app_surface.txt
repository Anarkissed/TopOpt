The app rounds for display; every underlying value is core's. This is the
point of routing the whole readout through ONE bridge call onto the same
functions fill_fit_region_cell uses: an app-side copy of the octet strut law
was 1.4x off core's once, and a control whose readout disagrees with the run
is worse than no readout.

Note the Auto row: 14 % gives a 0.42 mm strut — EXACTLY the profile's
extrusion width. That is what 'the lightest density that prints here' means,
and it is why the valid range starts there.

WHAT THIS IS NOT: a device screenshot. These are the model's own outputs,
which decide every number and word on those rows, rendered through the real
bridge. The section has NOT been exercised on a device or simulator.
