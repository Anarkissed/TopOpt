== S4 — DOES A PARAMETRIC DESIGN STILL LATTICE? YES, WITH ONE NAMED CAVEAT. ==

Two `lattice-variant` runs, HIS lattice and grading blocks lifted verbatim from
evidence/2026-08-07-lattice-recipe-not-triangles/job_his_2mm_skinnone.json,
differing ONLY in which design.bin they name. Rung 0.68 on both.

-- PASS 1: THE PARAMETRIC DESIGN IS REFUSED, AND THE REFUSAL IS CORRECT

  "the void space inside this lattice does not reach the exterior. 7 of 215
   lattice cells (68 of 1167 latticed voxels) sit in 1 SEALED cavity holding
   337.206 mm^3 of trapped space with no path out of the part."

SIMP's rung 0.68, same recipe, latticed and was ACCEPTED. So this is a property
of the DESIGN, not of the pipeline.

★ AND IT IS THE DIRECT CONSEQUENCE OF THE THING THAT MAKES THE METHOD WORTH
HAVING. PR 324's case for dropping the SIMP seed was that a coefficient in the
middle of solid material can be driven negative ON ITS OWN and open a hole there
— which a per-voxel phi cannot do, because its velocity is zero away from the
band. A hole opened in the INTERIOR *is* a sealed cavity until it merges with
the outside. On this part seven cells' worth never merged.

The check did its job exactly: it named the cavity, gave its bounding box and
volume, refused to auto-correct geometry nobody asked it to change, and stated
both ways out. Nothing about the grading law, the boundary rule, the
cells-per-member floor or the sub-floor predicate is implicated.

-- PASS 2: WITH require_lattice_void_reaches_exterior=false, IT LATTICES AND
   CERTIFIES. So the cavity is the ONLY thing between a parametric design and a
   latticed part.

★ THE MARGIN REPRODUCTION IS EXACT (3297.3 == 3297.3). `lattice-variant` REFUSES
to lattice a design whose recorded margin does not reproduce within
100 x cg_tolerance; the parametric design.bin round-trips through the store, the
grid and the load case with no drift at all. That is the strongest single check
in this task that the parametric path's certificate belongs to its own field.

★ AND THE TWO LATTICES LAND IN THE SAME PLACE: 1167 against 1221 latticed voxels,
lattice margin 3093 against 3090.49, effective 640.4 against 639.926, both OUT OF
REGIME on strut strength (a REPORT, not a gate, on both). Nothing in the lattice
path behaves differently on a parametric field.

-- SO, AGAINST THE TASK'S BLOCKED-STOP ("a PLSM design cannot be latticed without
   changing the grading law"): NOT TRIGGERED. The grading law is untouched. What
   IS triggered is a manufacturability refusal that SIMP does not hit, and it
   needs an answer before a from-scratch parametric part can be latticed by
   default.

   Candidates, cheapest first:
     (a) drain the cavity geometrically after optimisation;
     (b) PR 325's PERIMETER PENALTY — the one term that prices interface AREA,
         never tried on a parametric phi, and there is no reinitialisation there
         to fight it;
     (c) run the void-reachability walk INSIDE the optimiser so cavities never
         form. Only (c) prevents it, and it is the expensive one.

   Setting require_lattice_void_reaches_exterior=false ships trapped powder or
   resin. It is a trade to price, not a fix.
