R6 — ITERATIONS AND WALL, ALWAYS BOTH, SEPARATELY. And the file size, which is the
cost this mode actually incurs.

WHAT ISOLATES WHAT. On a graded two-step run the cell law runs AFTER the solve, so the
optimizer's trajectory cannot depend on the cell mode. The SOLVER ITERATIONS must
therefore be identical between two runs that differ only in the cell, and only the
lattice GENERATION and the emitted file may move. That control is asserted below; it
is what makes the wall-clock number mean "generation" rather than "a different design".

=== FIXTURE 1: one 12 mm include region, l-bracket, resolution 40, one rung ===
Chosen so BOTH modes could run: at a 0.42 mm bead `auto` plans 4.6026 mm (2.61 cells
across a 12 mm region — it percolates, so the pre-flight reports rather than refuses)
and `fit` derives 12/5 = 2.4000 mm.

auto (4.6026 mm)       cell None     latticed None   iters   40  gen_s 0              tris         0  bytes           0
fit  (2.4000 mm)       cell 2.4      latticed 635    iters   40  gen_s 0.09599854192  tris    168124  bytes     8406284

SOLVER ITERATIONS IDENTICAL: True  (40 vs 40)
AUTO EMITTED NOTHING: 12 mm / 4.6026 mm = 2.61 cells, under the 5-cell accuracy
floor, so every candidate fell back to solid and no lattice mesh exists. There is
no generation cost to compare against, and that IS the comparison: at a region
size where fit lattices 635 of 635 candidate voxels, auto lattices none.

=== FIXTURE 2: the SAME job at two hand-set cells (R1 cases C and D, res 24) ===
Both emit, so the cost ratio is measurable with the design held fixed.

fixed 2.5 mm           cell 2.5      latticed 550    iters   24  gen_s 0.1424933751   tris    437212  bytes    21860684
fixed 1.0 mm           cell 1        latticed 1168   iters   24  gen_s 0.3886541664   tris   1675088  bytes    83754484

SOLVER ITERATIONS IDENTICAL: True  (24 vs 24)
cell            2.5000 -> 1.0000 mm   (2.50x finer per axis)
triangles       437212 -> 1675088   (3.83x)
bytes           21860684 -> 83754484   (3.83x)
generation wall 0.142 s -> 0.389 s   (2.73x)

=== THE COST, STATED PLAINLY ===
A 2.50x finer cell cost 3.83x the triangles and 2.73x the generation wall on this
fixture. It is not the cube of the cell ratio because the latticed VOLUME also changes
(a finer cell clears the cells-per-member floor in more members, so more of the part is
latticed: 550 -> 1168 voxels here).

At the finest printable cell the absolute numbers get large. Measured on this branch:
  * 1.0950 mm on the seam fixture (l-bracket, res 40): 1,416,356 triangles, 70.8 MB
  * 1.0950 mm on the seven-4-mm-region job (l-bracket, res 48): 152.3 MB for the
    heaviest rung's lattice mesh (variant_068_lattice.stl)
That is arithmetic, not a defect — an octet cell carries ~24 struts and a 4.2x finer
cell puts ~4.2x more of them along every axis — but it is the practical cost of asking
for a cell that fits a 4 mm wall.

NOTE ON THE WALL NUMBERS. This machine was heavily loaded by other work while these ran
(load average above 40 on 8 cores), so the ABSOLUTE seconds are not a clean benchmark.
The RATIOS are between two runs measured under the same conditions minutes apart, and
the iteration counts — which are load-independent — are identical, which is the control
this bar actually needs.
