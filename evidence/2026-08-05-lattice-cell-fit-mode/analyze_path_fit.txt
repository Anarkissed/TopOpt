READ THE TWO 'out of regime' NUMBERS CAREFULLY — they answer different questions
and they are both right:
  * the PER-REGION flag is judged on the region's DECLARED extent (4 mm), which
    is what the cell was derived from and is the conservative reading.
  * fit.out_of_regime_voxels is judged per voxel on the DESIGN's own measured
    local member width. A 4 mm include slab cut into a thicker wall sits on
    material wider than 4 mm, so those voxels clear the accuracy floor at the
    derived cell even though the region as declared does not.
  * no_derivation_voxels is large here because the analyze path grades the WHOLE
    printed design (it passes no region mask), so every voxel outside the seven
    declared regions has no derived cell and stays solid, counted rather than
    guessed at.
