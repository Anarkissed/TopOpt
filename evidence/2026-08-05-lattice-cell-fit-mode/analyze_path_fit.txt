=== WHAT THESE NUMBERS SAY ===

1. AT HIS RESOLUTION, FIT LATTICES 4,414 VOXELS INSIDE HIS SEVEN DECLARED REGIONS where
   `auto` lattices none (auto refuses at the pre-flight, 0.87 cells across).

2. NOTHING INSIDE A DECLARED REGION WAS SKIPPED. candidates == latticed in all seven
   regions, at both resolutions. Every voxel that reached the fit path with a
   declaration covering it got a cell and was latticed.

3. THE SKIPPED VOXELS WERE ALL OUTSIDE. The identity
   no_derivation == printed_outside_regions holds exactly at both resolutions
   (41,877 at 128; 5,254 at 64). The analyze call site passes `nullptr` for the region
   mask (run_job.cpp:4497) — it grades the whole printed design by construction — so
   fit declines to invent cells for undeclared material, counts it, and keeps it solid.
   The OPTIMIZE path, whose candidate set IS include-scoped, reports no_derivation 0.

4. ★ distinct_cells = 1. All seven of his regions are 4 mm, so all seven derive the
   SAME 1.0950 mm cell. The multi-cell seam is NEVER exercised on his geometry; it is
   measured only on the constructed two-region fixture (s3_seam.txt).

5. ★ out_of_regime_voxels = 0 AT 128 while every REGION is stamped out of regime, and
   that is not a contradiction — the two are measured against different things:
     * the per-REGION flag uses the region's DECLARED extent (4 mm), which is what the
       cell was derived from, and is the conservative reading;
     * the per-VOXEL count uses the DESIGN's own measured local member width. A 4 mm
       include slab cut into a thicker wall sits on material wider than 4 mm, so those
       voxels clear the accuracy floor at the derived cell.
   At 64 the same run reports 0 as well. The gap between declared extent and measured
   width is the coherence gap PR #298 filed (§item-7b); it is reported here, not closed.
