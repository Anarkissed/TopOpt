READ IT LIKE THIS.
  FIRST TABLE (8a/8b) — d_margin is how far the CERTIFIED margin moves
  when a region carrying region_vM% of the part peak is latticed at 1.33
  cells per member instead of left solid, and `argmax` is whether the
  peak stress changed WHERE it is (the non-local effect, 8b).

  SECOND TABLE — the margin at a FIXED rho with the cell swept across the
  floor. The homogenized tensor C is a function of rho ALONE; cell size
  never enters the composite solve. So if these rows are identical, the
  certification is STRUCTURALLY BLIND to cells-per-member, and no d_margin
  in the first table — however small — is evidence that a sub-floor
  lattice is ACCURATE. It is only evidence that substituting C(rho) for
  solid did not move this part's margin. The accuracy question the floor
  exists to answer needs direct FEA of the real strut geometry, which the
  lattice Phase-0 probe measured at a 44-276x cost ceiling.
