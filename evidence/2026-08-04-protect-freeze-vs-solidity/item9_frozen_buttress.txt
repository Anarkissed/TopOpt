READ IT LIKE THIS.
  `adjacent` is printed voxels within 5 mm of the frozen wall's face —
  where a buttress goes. `reach_mm` is how far the added material extends.
  B is the question's configuration; C is the control that separates "the
  optimizer will not buttress" from "the FREEZE stopped it": if C places
  material beside the wall and B does not, the frozen region's zero
  sensitivity is starving the neighbours' signal. If B places it too, the
  freeze is exonerated — the adjoint still carries the frozen wall's own
  compliance contribution into its neighbours' sensitivities, which is
  what makes buttressing reachable at all.
  At a LOOSE budget both saturate and the comparison is uninformative;
  read the tight-budget rows.
