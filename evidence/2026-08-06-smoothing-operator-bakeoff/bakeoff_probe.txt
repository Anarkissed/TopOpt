== S3.3 THE INSTRUMENTS, VALIDATED ==
Every instrument below is checked against a fixture whose answer is
known by construction. An instrument that has never been shown to
MOVE cannot be trusted to report that something did not.

-- minimum cross-section: a dumbbell with a neck of known width --
The measure has a 2-voxel quantum (2*r*spacing, r integer), so the
sweep uses EVEN necks it can actually represent. An odd neck reads
down to the next even value and that is a property of the instrument,
not of the part.

THE `exported mm2` COLUMN IS NOT THE SAME NUMBER, AND THAT IS A
PRE-EXISTING CORE DEFECT, NOT AN OPERATOR EFFECT. Round-tripping the
solid through marching_cubes and voxelize_onto_grid (voxel.hpp:121)
ADDS solid voxels the source never had: on the neck-6 dumbbell,
1888 source voxels become 1932, and the 44 extra ones sit in columns
at (10,11,k) and (17,11,k) running from the bridge down to k = 0,
through space the source marks VOID. Those columns are the exact
corner columns of an axis-aligned bridge, which is the signature of
a parity/ray fill flipping on a degenerate edge hit. They are
CONNECTED to the part, so a largest-component filter does not remove
them, and because this statistic is a minimum they set a floor.
Consequence, stated plainly: the ABSOLUTE cross-section readings in
S3.4 carry that floor, so only the CHANGE from the exported baseline
is read there, and the solid-voxel count is printed beside it so a
change in the fill itself cannot masquerade as a change in the part.

-- maximum outward excursion: a constructed violation --
  wholly inside the box :  -1.0000 mm (must be <= 0)
  one vertex 0.75 mm out:  +0.7500 mm (must read 0.75)
instrument separates the two: YES

== S3.4 THE NUMBERS THAT DECIDE IT, ON A FIXTURE WITH A TENDRIL ==
The sphere has no tendril, so the minimum cross-section cannot be
exercised on it. The dumbbell can: a 2-voxel neck is the thinnest
member and is exactly what a smoother necks. That neck also sits ON
the 2-voxel floor §7 V3 gates at, so classify_trust_sign reads it as
material that matters and C2 makes it OUTWARD-ONLY. The design box
clips ONE side only, so the InwardOnly and Both cases are present
too rather than the box swallowing the whole part.

trust radius 0.2500 mm (0.5 cell), so the box bind tolerance is its
cube diagonal 0.4330 mm — see the note in the source.
C2 classification of 4384 vertices: 128 OutwardOnly, 480 InwardOnly, 0 Pinned, 3776 Both

`TENDRIL` is the minimum cross-section OF THE BRIDGE ITSELF (x-slices over
i in [10,18)) — the number his objection is about. `whole part` is the same
statistic over the entire grid, and its LOCATION is printed because the fill
defect documented in S3.3 puts an artefact column at z = 0 that can be thinner
than any real member.
