# The sheet laws per member width (gyroid, Schwarz-D)

Sheets spec 2026-11-25 rev 3, section 3.2, builder D. His word: "Measure thinner walls." The question:
can a Structural sheet be credited in a member thinner than today's 10 (gyroid) / 9 (Schwarz-D) cells
across, and from how few cells: 2 if the 2-cell rows come out steady, else 3.

Two measurements per member width N (cells across):

- **Strength:** `lattice_dehomog_probe widths` and `fitw`, giving `<type>/kfit_widths.csv` (the law rows)
  and `<type>/kcheck_widths.csv` (every pitch).
- **Bending stiffness:** `tpms_probe bend`, giving `bend/<type>_vf<rho>_vpc<v>_nc<...>/`.

Core carries both as tables (`core/src/fea/sheet_width_law_tables.inc`, generated by `gen_tables.py`
from these CSVs; `core/include/topopt/sheet_width_law.hpp`). `test_sheet_width_law` pins every row.
Published laws are a cross-check only; none is a row here.

## Method: 11-09's, unchanged in kind

`evidence/2026-11-09-lattice-type-strut-laws` (README, reproduce.sh) measured today's law. The same
probe, the same production wall (R7), the same steps:

- the exact K_dev (the 5x5 generalized eigenproblem), K_vol and the interlayer pair;
- bulk, the periodic cell, finest vpc 48;
- the boundary populations: the sandwich block (free lateral faces) and the cut-layer block;
- per density the envelope (max) of bulk and boundary;
- the aliasing guard (>15 % off the library density drops a fixture), and a dropped fixture is
  re-measured one pitch finer;
- every density row of each type's band;
- selfcheck S1-S6.

**What is new, and why:**

1. **Width.** The blocks are N = 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8 cells across (11-09: 4, and 3 for the thin rows).
   - Along the gripped axis a block is N long (11-09's cube) but at least 3. A 2-cell cube has no
     cell off the grips.
2. **Cut placements.** The cut layer is cut at 0.25, 0.5, 0.6 (11-09's) and 0.75 of a cell.
3. **The re-measure ladder keeps the width.** It goes vpc 16 -> 24 -> 32 -> 40 at the same N.
   11-09's ladder also stepped N4 -> N3, which here would change the very thing measured.
4. **One law pitch per fixture.** Each fixture is read at the coarsest pitch the guard keeps, which
   is 11-09's rule: vpc 16, and 24 for gyroid 0.150 (rho < 0.19) and Schwarz-D 0.200 (whose vpc16
   blocks fall apart, as on 11-09).
   - So every width of a density row is read at one pitch.
   - Finer pitches of the same fixtures are **checks** (kcheck_widths.csv), never law. A width
     measured finer must not read worse only for the extra resolution: the cut layer's grip corner
     rises with pitch (finding 2).
5. **A narrower member is never credited more than a wider one.** Per density, each coefficient is
   the max over the widths >= N. It is never under today's row (11-09's kfit.csv, the law from
   10 / 9 cells).
6. **Matrix-free solves.** `TOPOPT_DEHOMOG_MATFREE=1` solves the same reduced system with the same
   stopping criterion. An 8-cell block's assembled K alone would be several GB.
   - The 4-cell vpc16 blocks reproduce 11-09's K to every printed digit: cut 0.6 Kd 23.8671, today's
     law row; free surface 10.6999.
   - The bulk and selfcheck CSVs are byte-identical to 11-09's.
7. **One diagnostic population, not in the law.** `cut_off_grip`: the cut layer's cells off the two
   grip columns (see finding 2). `Kd_og` in kfit_widths.csv is the envelope with it in place of the
   cut layer, for the reviewer.

**Steady**, fixed in the timing commit c78d7c6d before the long runs (steady.py applies it):

- **a. Connected:** every 2-cell fixture at the law pitch is kept by the guard.
- **b. Width:** the 2-cell envelope is at most 1.25 x the 3-cell one, by 11-09's populations and off
  the grips.
- **c. Pitch:** at each pitch the 2-cell free-surface Kd is within 10 % of the 3-cell one.
- **d. Placement:** the 2-cell spread over the placements is at most 1.25 x the 3-cell spread.
- **e. Bending:** the 2-cell bending error at the three densities is within +-20 % of its mean
  (at least +-5 points).

Steady gives a floor of 2; otherwise 3.

## Findings

1. **Kd rises as the member WIDENS, at every density, for both sheets.**
   - At one pitch, every population rises from 2 cells to 8: free surface, 11-09's cut layer, and the
     cut layer off the grips (`kcheck_widths.csv`).
   - The 8-cell blocks read 1.6 to 1.75 x the 2-cell ones.
   - The rule "a narrower member is never credited more than a wider one" therefore makes every row
     the 8-cell value, 1.3 to 2.8 x today's.
   - In these fixtures the risk does not grow as the member narrows; it grows with the width of the
     TEST BLOCK.
2. **11-09's cut layer includes the cut cells beside the gripped faces, and its peak sits there.**
   - `classify` returns Cut before it checks the grip columns. 11-09 leaves the grip population itself
     out of the law; it keeps these.
   - The diagnostic `cut_off_grip` gives the same cells off the two grip columns. Gyroid 0.313, 8
     cells, cut 0.25: 39.0 in the cut layer against 13.0 off the grips (free surface 13.9).
   - The corner rises with pitch and does not settle: at 3 cells, 27 / 38 / 57 at vpc 16 / 32 / 48.
   - Off the grips it settles, except at the 0.6 cut, which leaves a thin sliver: 13 / 31 / 27 there,
     about 10 to 12.6 at the other cuts.
   - The corner also grows with the block's width. The likely reason: in a wider block the first cells
     sit relatively closer to the macro edge where a gripped face meets a free one. That is finding 1's
     mechanism.
   - Off the grips, every row is below today's: gyroid 39.0 / 38.1 / 17.0 / 9.84 / 7.27, Schwarz-D
     24.7 / 13.2 / 9.78 / 5.87 (`Kd_og`).
3. **By its own method, today's law reads low.**
   - The 4-cell block cut at 0.6 reproduces today's gyroid 0.313 row exactly (23.8671).
   - The same block cut at 0.5 gives 31.1; the 8-cell block gives 42.1.
   - So the law from 10 / 9 cells, by 11-09's populations, would be at least the 8-cell value. It is
     not changed here: that is TOReview's and builder F's ground.
   - The choice for TOReview: keep 11-09's populations (every row, and today's, at the wider value), or
     leave the grip-adjacent cut cells out, as the grip population is (`Kd_og`: lower for every row,
     today's included).
4. **The free surface behaves.**
   - It grows with pitch as the bulk does: 3 cells 9.6 / 11.4 / 12.6 at vpc 16 / 32 / 48, bulk 10.2 /
     12.4 / 13.7.
   - The 2- and 3-cell blocks read alike at every pitch: K2/K3 0.96 to 0.99.
5. **Bending.**
   - The averaged material is too stiff at every width and density, so core scales it down.
   - The gyroid's error grows as the density falls: 2 cells +55.4 / +43.6 / +39.8 % at 0.146 / 0.405 /
     0.600.
   - Schwarz-D's does not depend on the density: +20.1 / +20.5 / +20.7 %.

## Steady: the 2-cell rows (steady.csv, steady.txt)

All 38 lines pass for both sheets: criteria a-d at every density row, and e. **Measured floor: 2 cells**
for the gyroid and for Schwarz-D (`sheet_width_floor_measured`). In words:

- The 2-cell fixtures are all kept by the guard.
- The 2-cell envelope reads 0.89 to 0.93 x the 3-cell one by 11-09's populations, and 0.955 to 1.00 x
  off the grips.
- The 2-cell free surface reads 0.955 to 0.99 x the 3-cell one at every pitch measured.
- The 2-cell spread over the placements is the 3-cell one's to within 0.08.

The gyroid's bending passes narrowly:

- Its 2-cell error is +55.40 % at the band floor, against a band of +-9.25 points around the mean
  +46.26 %. The edge is 55.51, so it passes by 0.11 point.
- That floor wall is 1.13 voxels at vpc 24. Batch B's finer-pitch bending check (vpc 36 at the band floor, vpc 32 at the ceiling, 2-4 cells)
  did not run (below). So whether the voxel wall inflates the floor's 55.40 is not measured.

A floor of 2 means walls from 8.7 mm (gyroid) and 10.8 mm (Schwarz-D) at the smallest cells (spec 3.2).
The lead and TOReview set the number.

## Six voxels across the wall

**The law rows do not reach it.** At 11-09's pitch the median wall run is 2-5 voxels and the true wall
1.0-3.3 voxels, as 11-09's boundary rows were (their caveat 1: lower bounds).

**Six cannot be reached within the Mac time.**
- Six voxels of true wall would need vpc 30 (gyroid 0.60) up to vpc 120 (gyroid 0.15).
- An 8-cell block at vpc 60 is about 10^8 voxels.
- The 8-cell blocks set every row (finding 1), so no row could be measured there.

**Measured at 6 or more** (median run; true wall in brackets):
- Gyroid 0.313 at 3 cells, vpc 48: 8 voxels (4.9). Free surface 12.6; off the grips 10.6-12.6, and
  26.7 at the 0.6 sliver; the grip corner 43-57.
- Not run: batch B's 2-cell vpc 48 blocks, which reach 6 at gyroid 0.313-0.601 and Schwarz-D
  0.313-0.600.

## Mac time and HEAVY.lock

Each run took the lock with noclobber, one line naming its pid, and removed only its own line.
Machine: Apple M2 Pro, 6P + 4E cores, 16 GB. Every law solve ran single-threaded (deterministic);
6 to 10 processes ran side by side.

**From batch A on, every job started through a memory gate** (the standing order of 11-25; the timing row ran before it):
- It started only if its estimated peak plus 3 GB fitted in what macOS reported free.
- No memory alarm fired; the largest probes (the 8-cell vpc24 blocks) peaked at about 2.8 GB with
  compressed memory.

## Caveats

1. **Lower bounds** (11-09's caveat 1, carried).
   - The law rows are read at 11-09's pitch, with 1.0-3.3 voxels of true wall, and the peak still rises
     with pitch.
   - The grip corner that sets every row (finding 2) does not settle with pitch at all.
2. **The interlayer factor 0.55 is unsourced** (11-09's caveat 3). The interlayer law was measured with
   the build direction on a cube axis; a tilted face frame is outside it.
3. **The blocks.**
   - A width-N block is N x N cells across, N long along the gripped axis (3 for N = 2).
   - A member of the part is not a cube. Neither the cube's own aspect nor the grip faces are the
     part's; finding 1 is a property of this fixture.
   - Width N means N whole cells. The cut blocks are N + 0.25 to N + 0.75 cells across in one direction
     and sample where a wall cuts the grid.
4. **The bending band-floor rows** have 1.13 voxels of wall (gyroid) and 1.26 (Schwarz-D) at vpc 24.
   - The error is a ratio of two solves on the same voxels.
   - The finer-pitch bending check: not run (batch B).
5. **11-09's member-width guard gap** (its caveat 2: the outside of the grid is not counted as void)
   is unchanged.
6. **Published laws** were not used as rows.

## Files

- `reproduce.sh`: builds, runs every job list (`jobs_1_timing.txt`, `jobs_2_A_*.txt`,
  `jobs_3_B_*.txt`), gathers, fits, applies the criteria, and writes core's table.
- `steady.py`: the five criteria.
- `gen_tables.py`: writes core/src/fea/sheet_width_law_tables.inc.
- `<type>/`:
  - `selfcheck.csv` / `.log`;
  - `bulk_tensor.csv`, `k_exact_bulk.csv`, `k_states_bulk.csv`, `bulk.log` (byte-identical to 11-09's);
  - `parts/` (one exact and one states CSV per job), `logs/` (one per job, with /usr/bin/time);
  - `k_exact_w_all.csv` and `k_states_w_all.csv` (gathered);
  - `k_cells_w_all.csv.gz` (per-cell records, compressed);
  - `kfit_widths.csv` (the law rows), `kcheck_widths.csv` (every pitch), `fitw.log`.
- `bend/<type>_vf<rho>_vpc<v>_nc<...>/`: `<type>_bend.csv` and `.txt`.
- `jobs_done*.log`: every job's end, wall time and exit code.

The binaries that ran: `lattice_dehomog_probe` and `tpms_probe` built from f989ce26.
- `widths`, `selfcheck`, `bulk` and `bend` are unchanged since.
- `fitw` was rewritten in e32b2c47 to read the law pitch. It only reads CSVs.
