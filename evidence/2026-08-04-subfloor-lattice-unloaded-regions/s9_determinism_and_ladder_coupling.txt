=== S9 — DETERMINISM, and the LADDER COUPLING it was run to attribute ===
(task 2026-08-04-subfloor-lattice-unloaded-regions)

THE OBSERVATION THAT FORCED THIS CONTROL. On the maintainer's part with the
lattice scoped to his one qualifying wall, arming retention changed the
SOLID margin of rungs BELOW the one where retention fired — rungs at which
nothing was retained at all. Retention is a lattice post-process and cannot
touch the solid ladder, so either the pipeline is not run-to-run
deterministic on this part, or arming retention perturbs later rungs
through something shared. This control decides which.

--- CONTROL: the SAME job (retention OFF) run TWICE ---
  rung 0.68: densities bit-identical=True  classification flips=0
  rung 0.52: densities bit-identical=True  classification flips=0
  rung 0.38: densities bit-identical=True  classification flips=0
  rung 0.26: densities bit-identical=True  classification flips=0
  report.json  run1=992fe935be51352a  run2=992fe935be51352a  IDENTICAL
  fields.bin   run1=5002bab24ecd0909  run2=5002bab24ecd0909  IDENTICAL
  design.bin   run1=9535f13859e8cfe5  run2=9535f13859e8cfe5  IDENTICAL

  S9: MET — two runs of the same job are byte-identical, so the pipeline
       is deterministic on this part and any difference below is CAUSED.

--- THE EFFECT: retention OFF vs ON, same job otherwise ---
  rung 0.68: densities bit-identical=True  classification flips=0
  rung 0.52: densities bit-identical=False  classification flips=40
  rung 0.38: densities bit-identical=False  classification flips=380
  rung 0.26: densities bit-identical=False  classification flips=416

READ IT LIKE THIS. Rung 0.68 — the rung where retention actually fired — is
BIT-IDENTICAL in its density: retention is a post-process and it did not
touch the design it was applied to. The rungs BELOW it diverge. Since the
control above shows the pipeline is deterministic, that divergence is caused
by arming retention, and the only channel between a finished rung's lattice
work and the next rung's optimize is solver state carried across solves
(the warm start, handoff 110). The composite margin moved by at most
0.0853%, no verdict flipped on any rung, and the argmax did not move — so
this is not a blocked-stop by any criterion this task set. It is, however, a
real coupling that nothing in the codebase documents, and it is filed as
such rather than left for someone to rediscover.
