--- 2. THE DEFECT, before the fix ---
  retention OFF vs ON (pre-fix binary):
    rung 0.68: identical=True  classification flips=0
    rung 0.52: identical=False  classification flips=40
    rung 0.38: identical=False  classification flips=380
    rung 0.26: identical=False  classification flips=416

  Rung 0.68 — the rung retention actually FIRED on — is bit-identical: the
  lattice is a post-process and it did not touch its own design. The rungs
  AFTER it diverge. Cause: lattice_one_variant runs from run_job's on_variant
  callback, i.e. BETWEEN rungs, and its FEA solves were harvesting into and
  dropping the Krylov recycle subspace (krylov_recycling=true, dim 16,
  reset_per_rung=false) and the GenEO basis (basis_dim 1674) that rung k+1's
  optimize consumes.
