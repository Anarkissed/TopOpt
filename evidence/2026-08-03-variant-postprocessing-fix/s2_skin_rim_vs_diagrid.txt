A diagrid anchors at the LANDINGS of clipped interior struts. With no interior
struts there are no landings, so there is no skin to emit — the same failure as
the maintainer's variant 038. A rim is worse still: it needs an analytic PLANE
face, and no production LatticeBoundary ever has one (see the handoff, S1).
