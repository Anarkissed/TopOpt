A 6x6x6 block of 8 mm cells at the band middle, clipped by a box whose x-min face sits at c cells
(c = 0.05 .. 0.95), Diagrid skin. Each entry is c:<pieces>/<anchors>: how many separate pieces the
skin leaves among the anchors on that face's interior (one cell in from its edges). 1 = connected.

Cause, from each type's canonical cell (crossing_node_gap in lattice_gen.cpp): near a plane of nodes
the landings bunch around the nodes that send a strut ACROSS it, and the diagrid must bridge the
gap between those nodes. Octet's link radius, 0.72 cell, is 1.8 % over octet's gap.
