READING (the answer to Q2's two questions): it is MIXED, and the sign depends on the direction.
At equal relative density the braces take material from the legs, so the full octet is SOFTER along
the cell axes and STIFFER along the body diagonal and in shear:
  * along a cell axis (E100) the legs-only tensor OVER-states the full octet by 11-22 % of the full
    value (full/legs - 1 = -10.6 / -13.6 / -18.2 % at rho 0.25 / 0.35 / 0.45) -- the UNSAFE direction
    for a member loaded along x, y or z;
  * along the body diagonal (E111) and in shear (C44) it UNDER-states it by 13-19 % -- conservative.
The gap widens with density. Shipped behaviour is unchanged by this report; whether octet moves to its
own rows (band 0.207-0.480, narrower than today's 0.05-0.90) is the maintainer's ruling (Q2).
