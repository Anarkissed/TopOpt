The STL and the 3MF of the same part run_job() to a BYTE-IDENTICAL result.
Because the voxel grid + tags are identical (see stl_vs_3mf_equivalence.txt),
every stage downstream -- the SIMP solve, the gate, the exported marching-cubes
meshes -- consumes identical input and produces identical output.
