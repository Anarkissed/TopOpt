MECHANISM (from the per-iteration CSVs):
  FIXED  max design step over the whole run = 0.1998 (the step is genuinely
         PINNED at the 0.2 move limit — confirms handoff 193).
  ADAPT  the move grows to the 0.5 ceiling, but the design step ACTUALLY
         exceeds 0.2 on only 13 of 645 iterations (max step 0.441), and every
         one of those 13 is in rung 2's high-β (β=4) structural-rearrangement
         stage — the one rung that saved iterations.
  So the "pinning" is largely coincidental: in the forming phase the natural MMA
  step magnitude is ~0.2, so enlarging the trust region leaves the step
  unchanged (the asymptote-bracketed convex subproblem optimum is interior to
  the enlarged box). Releasing the move only accelerates the small subset of
  iterations where the subproblem genuinely wants a larger step. Outer-iteration
  COUNT is set by the plateau detector + β-continuation structure, not the
  per-step trust-region radius.
