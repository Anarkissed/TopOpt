## E2.1: graded on the worst scenario, certified under each

**What was wrong (measured first, `e21_red_first.txt`).** On a job with load scenarios the lattice
was graded from the governing scenario's stress but certified under scenario 1 alone, whichever
governed. A 60 x 20 x 20 mm bar with a light load listed first and a heavy one second:

| path | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| run (the lattice beside each version) | lattice margin **138.3** ACCEPTED, under the light load; the solid governed at 8.02 under the heavy one | **7.22**, under the heavy load; per scenario 138.3 (light) and 7.22 (heavy); the solid reproduces 8.02 exactly |
| Lattice an optimized result (lattice_variant) | **refused**: "does NOT reproduce the margin the run recorded" (recorded 4.95, reproduced 77.95) | reproduces; latticed again with the heavy scenario governing (test §4) |
| Lattice the original part (lattice_part) | certified under scenario 1 | graded and certified on both (test §5) |
| an analyze job with a grading block | re-certified under scenario 1 | re-certified under each scenario the lattice uses |

**What changed** (`run_job.cpp`, `job.cpp`, `job.hpp`, `observability.*`):
- `lattice.scenarios` parses as above.
- `lattice_grading_stress`: per voxel, the worst von Mises over the scenarios the lattice uses. The
  governing scenario's field is the variant's own double field; the others are the float32 copies
  the views carry. A lattice on the governing scenario alone grades from exactly the field it
  always did, and a job without scenarios gets `v.von_mises_field` by reference.
- `LatticeCertContext::scenarios`: the scenarios sized on the run's whole list (the weak springs
  scale with the heaviest load in the list), then filtered to the ones the lattice uses.
- `certify_posture`: one posture certified under each scenario; the worst governs by
  `certify_scenarios`' own rule, and the verdict is accepted only if every scenario's own analysis
  is accepted and none is unheld. Used for the solid re-certification, the lattice certificate and
  the clamp counterfactual. The organic certificates get one load case per scenario
  (`organic_load_cases`).
- The solid re-certification compares each scenario's margin with the one the run recorded for it.
- lattice_variant / lattice_part re-certify the stored design under every scenario, the way the run
  did, and hand each scenario's field to the lattice.
- Edits inside the shared `lattice_one_variant` were posted to TO-core before they were made
  (19:48Z and 19:58Z): the grading reads, the two `lattice_cert_context` calls, the organic load
  cases, the counterfactual and the receipt call. TO-core's lines are untouched.

## E2.5: two regions on one face (`7445bb6c`)

Ruling 4's owner rule gives a point two include prisms share to the region with the nearest
face plane, and an exact tie to the lower declared id. Two regions on ONE face tie everywhere
they overlap, so the whole overlap went to whichever was declared first.

- `include_wins_plane_tie` (`stepped_plan.cpp`): on one face plane (coplanar, facing the same
  way) the DEEPER region wins, the plan's approved rule; at equal depth, and on every other tie,
  the lower id wins as before. `stepped_region_owner` and the owned-region clip term in
  `LatticeBoundary` both call it, so the voxels and the struts agree. A tie between two
  different faces (at a corner) is unchanged.
- The depth tie at the parse: with two regions on a protected face, the DEEPEST must equal the
  protection; a shallower one lies inside that slab.
- **For the app (B6/B7):** the preview's owner twin must take the same tie-break, or the preview
  and the run will disagree on a face with two lattice groups.

Measured (fit cells, `test_lattice_whole_part` §5-§6): a 10 mm prism at 0.75 and a 16 mm one at
0.88 on the top face lay 0.88 throughout in both declaration orders; the 10 mm one alone lays
0.75. At the gate, on `4e7648d8`'s CLI the shallow-first order laid 0.75-0.88 (the overlap to the first declared) and the deep-first order 0.88; with the fix both lay 0.88 (`e25_two_regions.txt`).


## E3: an include region's own grading (`4ace41f5`)

Each value falls back to the part's `grading.*`. They are refused on an exclude region, which
has no lattice to grade. Measured on the bar latticed as the part, with "- Lattice faces"
and a 10 mm region on the top face (`test_lattice_region_grading`):

| region setting | region's graded densities | part's lattice mass |
|---|---|---|
| none of its own | (the part's grade) | 7.853 g |
| `grading: "stress"` (the part's own, the control) | 0.1654-0.6710, same file byte for byte | 7.853 g |
| `rho_min 0.5, rho_max 0.7` | 0.5000-0.5217 (low stress sits at the band's floor) | 8.637 g |
| `grading: "off"` | 0.6710 throughout (its graded peak) | 9.334 g |
| `demand_exponent: 3` | 0.1415-0.3728 | 7.099 g |
| `shape_grade: true` (the part's off) | 192 of 256 voxels still latticed: 64 turned solid | 8.619 g |

- `grade_lattice` (shared, posted first) takes a per-voxel region id (ruling 4's owner over the
  includes, or over `lattice.whole_part`'s regions) and a per-region override table. The
  region's band and exponent replace the part's in the law. A one-density region ("off",
  "shape") takes its stated `relative_density`, else its graded peak, so it is at least as
  dense as its grade everywhere and certifies wherever the grade does. Without overrides the
  table is not handed over and the law is untouched.
- The outline beam walks each region's own face plane with its own switch and band.
- `lattice_regions` in the receipt reports each region's numbers, read from the law before the
  certificate's as-built rebuild. The rebuild reads each laid cell, and a cell straddling a
  region's edge carries its neighbour's density, so it is the wrong source for "this region's
  densities".
- Not built: the cell window per region (`cell_min_mm`, `cell_max_mm`), which needs the cell
  plan per region and is shared with the design-box work; the grade strength (below).
- **For the app (B7):** an Auto "Off" with no density is the region's graded peak, not a search
  for the lightest one-density lattice. That search costs one certification solve per trial
  density; say if it is wanted.


## The swept cell plan: a balance-split block is never left mixed (`6c6102bc`)

TO-core's finding (20:37Z), given to LatticeTypes by the 21:40Z ruling. A swept graded
lattice_part of a solid block threw "plan_cell_sizes: level assignment is not an aligned
octree". The post-balance feasibility pass sets level -1 per BASE cell where a split made the
strut unprintable. A block split to L-1 whose cells needed different levels therefore ended
partly at L-1 and partly at -1, and the plan's own alignment check threw.

- Now a block left mixed is split one level more, its cells that fail there drop in turn, and
  the 2:1 balance re-runs until nothing changes. Every step only refines or drops, so it ends.
- A plan with no mixed block takes one pass that changes nothing but the drop it always made,
  so every plan that didn't throw is the plan it was (the 21 binaries below).
- The fit planner raises density instead and has no post-balance drop, so it is untouched.

- `test_cell_plan_mixed_block` (new, unit): one corner cell needing level 2 in a block the

### Two premises in my contract that the code does not support (need a ruling)

- **None and Covered are the same in the run.** The app sends None as `skin "none"` and Covered
  as `skin "none"` plus `outer_finish "shell"`, but `"shell"` is also the default, so the two
  jobs are identical and the run prints the same file. Before per-region finish (E2.4), the
  question is what None should be in core: an open lattice needs `outer_finish "skin"`, which
  today requires the diagrid and is refused by the certificate (`lattice_accepted` false).
- **The grade strength has nothing to scale in core.** The app's shape-fit band steps the
  cells down and thickens the struts near the outline, by `gradeAmount(strength)`. Core's
  `shape_grade` is a solid outline beam (beam plus a solid bleed only once the band passes
  25 mm), cleared out of the lattice. So a core `shape_grade_strength` would have no gradient to
  act on, and today the run already differs from the preview inside that band. E3 builds the
  band, exponent, grading mode and per-region shape grade without it.

### Findings on the way (not changed, outside E2)

1. **A lattice with no solid voxel certifies as margin inf, ACCEPTED.** `analyze.cpp` leaves a
   latticed voxel's stress out of the margin (strut strength is reported, not gated, by the
   2026-07-31 ruling), so a whole-part lattice has nothing to measure. Seen on an analyze job's
   re-check and on "Lattice the original part" with no region and skin "none" (the bar: 1,536 of
   1,536 voxels latticed, max effective von Mises 1.18 MPa, both scenario margins null, the first
   listed scenario "governing" on the tie, `strut_strength_uncertified` true). The run path keeps
   a shell solid, so it is finite there. Proposed: "not certified" instead of a margin when no
   solid voxel carries load (or arm the strut gate); a ruling.
2. **"Lattice the original part" with growth on lattices the whole growth box.** lattice_part
   builds the part's design from `cert_grid.solid()` over the solved grid. With
   `minimize_plastic` false the grid is the auto-derived growth box, and every box voxel counts,
   so the bar's file spans -5.6 to 65.6 mm and 2.84x the part; with growth off, 0 to 60 mm and
   1.00. The grading + design box refusal does not fire, because an auto-derived box is not
   `has_design_box`. Reported to TO-core (design box + lattice is its batch), 20:24Z; TO-core fixed it on
   claude/grading-design-box at 49048743 (lattice_part never expands the domain). My tests run
   lattice_part with growth off.
3. **run_info.json writes a bare `inf`** for a non-finite number (`observability.cpp`'s `fmt`,
   about 200 fields), which is not JSON. Seen on finding 1's `recertified_margin`. The new
   `recertified_scenarios` list writes null. Proposed: null everywhere (it changes those bytes).
4. **The part's shape grade does nothing on a lattice with no regions.** The outline beam's walk
   seeds only from voxels an include region owns, using that region's face plane, so a
   whole-part lattice with `grading.shape_grade` true turns 0 voxels solid (measured on the
   bar). E3's per-region beam works, because it walks the E3 regions. Whether a regionless
   part should get a beam at its own surface is a ruling.
5. **The swept cell plan threw "level assignment is not an aligned octree"** (TO-core's finding): **fixed in `6c6102bc`**, below.
